01关于About

用更少的比特，表达更有价值的视觉信息。Representing valuable visual information with fewer bits.

我是武汉理工大学计算机技术专业硕士研究生，目前在周俊伟教授指导下开展图像压缩方向的研究。我关注如何在降低存储与传输成本的同时，保留对人类视觉和智能任务真正重要的信息。

I am a master's student in Computer Technology at Wuhan University of Technology. Under the supervision of Prof. Junwei Zhou, I study image compression and how to preserve information that matters to both human vision and intelligent tasks at lower storage and transmission costs.

除学术研究外，我也关注量化交易与可靠软件系统，通过工程实践积累数据分析、策略实现和系统验证经验。

Beyond academic research, I am interested in quantitative trading and reliable software systems, building practical experience in data analysis, strategy implementation, and system validation.

02研究Research

研究兴趣Research interests

R1

学习式图像压缩Learned Image Compression

探索数据驱动的图像编码、熵建模与重建方法。Exploring data-driven image coding, entropy modeling, and reconstruction.

R2

率失真优化Rate–Distortion Optimization

关注码率、重建质量与感知体验之间的平衡。Studying the balance among bitrate, reconstruction quality, and perception.

R3

高效视觉表征Efficient Visual Representation

关注面向人类与机器视觉任务的紧凑信息表示。Investigating compact representations for human and machine vision.

研究仍在持续推进；论文、实验结果与开源实现将在此更新。This work is ongoing. Publications, experimental results, and open-source implementations will be added here.
